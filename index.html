Frequently asked questions

What does Yurist AI do?

You type a legal question in plain language. Yurist AI uses an AI model and a collection of Uzbekistan laws (1,200+ legal documents) to explain which area of law applies, what the relevant legislation says, and where to read the official text on lex.uz.

Is Yurist AI a replacement for a lawyer?

No. Yurist AI gives general legal information, not legal advice, and its answers may be wrong or incomplete. Always check the official text and consult a qualified lawyer before making decisions that affect your rights.

Is Yurist AI connected to the government?

No. Yurist AI is an independent project and does not represent any government agency or official body.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You register with your name, email and password so your conversation history can be saved. Using the app is free and requires no payment details.

Where do my questions go?

Your questions are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our servers. To generate an answer, the text of your question is passed to a third-party AI provider (OpenAI). The app asks for your consent before your first message. Your name, email and password are not sent to the AI provider. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report an incorrect or harmful answer?

Tap the flag button (⚑) under any AI answer, choose a reason and send. You can also use the thumbs up and down buttons, or email us with the question and the answer you received.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted. You can also request deletion by email. See Delete account.

Which languages are supported?

The app interface is in Uzbek. You can ask questions in Uzbek or Russian.
