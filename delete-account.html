This page explains how to delete your Yurist AI account and the data associated with it.

Option 1: Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Yurist AI and sign in
  2. Open Settings (Sozlamalar)
  3. Under Account (Hisob), tap Delete account (Hisobni o'chirish)
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete (O'chirish)

Your account and all of its data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request by email

If you no longer have the app, email admin@yuristai.uz from the email address you registered with, using the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number and password
  • All of your conversations (questions and answers)
  • Your ratings and reports

We do not keep any of this data after deletion, except where the law requires us to.

Hisobni o'chirish (o'zbekcha)

Ilovada: Sozlamalar → Hisob → Hisobni o'chirish → tasdiqlang. Hisobingiz va unga tegishli barcha ma'lumotlar (profil, suhbatlar, baholar) darhol va butunlay o'chiriladi. Ilovasiz o'chirish uchun ro'yxatdan o'tgan emailingizdan admin@yuristai.uz manziliga "Hisobimni o'chiring" deb yozing.
